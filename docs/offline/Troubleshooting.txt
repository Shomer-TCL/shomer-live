TROUBLESHOOTING

Unknown command at Writing recovery:
The USB download may have succeeded while the phone rejected the actual write.
Do not treat that as a successful flash or test random other partitions.
Read fastboot --version; getvar product; getvar is-userspace; getvar unlocked;
getvar version-bootloader; getvar partition-size:recovery. Keep the full log.
An unsupported is-userspace variable is inconclusive; this flasher stops writes.

Bootloader fastboot vs fastbootd:
Both can appear in fastboot devices. is-userspace: no identifies bootloader;
yes identifies fastbootd. Unknown does not identify either.
Neutronscott's preloader handshake catches bootloader fastboot while powering on.
fastboot reboot fastboot requests recovery's fastbootd, then it must be verified.
adb reboot bootloader may boot normal Android on this handset; use the documented
powered-off preloader route if that happens. Do not assume a transition occurred.

BROM not found / MTKClient authentication failure:
Check physical cable, power-off/key sequence, USB enumeration and the tool log.
The phone may not expose BROM or accept the available download agent.
The guided backup stops and does not unlock or flash recovery automatically.

Backup incomplete:
Do not proceed on a false success assumption. Preserve partial files and logs.
Recovery backup requires successful stock recovery restore before other writes.
If restore failed, the recovery contents are uncertain and may still provide root.
Keep the phone private and diagnose the restore route first.

Backup location refused:
The live RAM filesystem, ISO filesystem and temporary loop-backed storage do not
retain data after shutdown. Mount a chosen physical drive and select a folder there.
For FAT32 use another filesystem when a dump or backup ZIP exceeds 4 GiB.
